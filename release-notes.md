# Release notes

## 1.0.0

First release.

### Catalogue system
- Index rail header: a numbered vertical index down the left edge on desktop, with submenus opening as a full-height panel beside it
- Mobile index: a numbered, ruled index strip under the mobile header, and a menu drawer laid out as the full index
- Ledger product cards: numbered pieces, a material line and a ruled price row, with grids ruled into one catalogue plate
- Plates and index views on collection and search pages, with the hovered piece's photo following the cursor in the index; articles and pages line up in the same numbered rows as products
- Contents list layout for the collections page: numbered, ruled rows with product counts and a cursor photo preview
- Catalogue product page: numbered figure labels, a type and SKU reference line and numbered product notes
- Order-slip cart: with ledger cards, the cart page and drawer show numbered lines, SKU references and a ruled summary
- Chapter section headings: a full-width rule and a running number beside each section's eyebrow
- Journal list layout for the blog and featured blog: numbered, ruled entries with the photo at the end of each row
- Colophon footer: a heavy top rule, ruled columns, numbered menu headings and the store's typefaces named in the bottom bar

### Home page and storytelling
- Slideshow and hero banner sections, with video, image-with-text, collage and collection tiles
- Featured collection, featured product and featured blog sections
- Shop the look and shoppable gallery with product hotspots
- Testimonials, timeline, FAQ, press logos, trust badges and marquee sections
- Before/after comparison slider, product comparison table and countdown timer
- Newsletter sign-up and newsletter popup, with a privacy consent checkbox

### Product page
- Media gallery with zoom
- Variant picker, quantity selector and sticky add-to-cart bar
- Pickup availability, Shop Pay Installments and unit prices
- Size guide, specifications, product personalization and bundle offer
- Delivery estimate, with an optional order-by countdown
- Product recommendations and recently viewed products
- Quick view and quick add from product cards

### Collection, search and cart
- Collection filtering and sorting, in a sidebar or a drawer
- Collection banner layout
- Predictive search and a search results page with filters
- Cart page and cart drawer, with a free shipping bar and suggestions

### Store-wide
- The Monograph preset, for jewelry and accessory brands
- Style settings for the header, footer, product page, product cards and section headings
- A panel and tag corner radius setting: square panels, tags and icon buttons at 0, rounded above it
- Color schemes, Shopify font picker and global typography settings
- Country/currency and language selectors
- Follow on Shop and payment icons in the footer
- Custom Liquid section and block; app blocks supported
- Right-to-left layout styles (no right-to-left translations are included)
- Contact, About, FAQ, blog, article, 404, password and gift card pages
