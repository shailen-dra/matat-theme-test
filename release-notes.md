# Release notes

## 1.0.0

First release.

### Home page and storytelling
- Slideshow and hero banner sections, with video, image-with-text, collage and collection tiles
- Featured collection, featured product and featured blog sections
- Shop the look and shoppable gallery with product hotspots
- Testimonials, timeline, FAQ, press logos, trust badges and marquee sections
- Before/after comparison slider, product comparison table and countdown timer
- Newsletter sign-up and newsletter popup, with a privacy consent checkbox

### Product page
- Media gallery with zoom
- Variant picker, quantity selector and sticky add-to-cart bar
- Pickup availability, Shop Pay Installments and unit prices
- Size guide, specifications, product personalization and bundle offer
- Product recommendations and recently viewed products
- Quick view and quick add from product cards

### Collection, search and cart
- Collection filtering and sorting, in a sidebar or a drawer
- Collection banner layout
- Predictive search and a search results page with filters
- Cart page and cart drawer, with upsells and discount codes

### Store-wide
- Color schemes, Shopify font picker and global typography settings
- Country/currency and language selectors
- Follow on Shop and payment icons in the footer
- Custom Liquid section and block; app blocks supported
- Right-to-left layout and a Hebrew translation
- Contact, About, FAQ, blog, article, 404, password and gift card pages
