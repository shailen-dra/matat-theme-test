# Blog and pages

## Blog (journal)

Open a blog page in the theme editor to edit the **Blog** section.

- **Layout:**
  - **Cards:** a grid of article cards, with **Columns on desktop**.
  - **Journal list:** numbered, ruled entries with the date, title and
    excerpt, and the article's photo at the end of each row. On mobile the
    photo becomes a small image beside the title, and the excerpt is hidden.
- **Articles per page**
- **Show topic filter:** links to filter articles by their tags.
- **Show featured image**, **Show date**, **Show author** and **Show excerpt**

To show recent articles on another page, add the **Blog posts** section,
which has the same two layouts. See [Home page sections](home-page-sections.md).

## Articles

The **Article** section shows the article's title, date, author, featured
image and content, with its tags at the end.

- **Show featured image**, **Show date** and **Show author**
- If comments are turned on for the blog (**Content → Blog posts → Manage
  blogs**), shoppers can leave comments below the article.

## Pages

The default page template shows the page title and content.

- **Content width:** **Narrow** or **Wide**.

To build a richer page, such as an About page, create a new page template in
the theme editor and add sections to it, for example Image with text,
Timeline, Comparison table or Store locations. Then assign the template to
the page in **Online Store → Pages**.

## Contact page

Assign the **contact** template to your contact page in **Online Store →
Pages**. Its **Contact form** section has a **Heading**, **Text** and
**Button label**. Messages are sent to your store's email address.

## FAQ

The **FAQ** section lists questions and answers as **Accordion** blocks.

- **Layout:** **Stacked**, or **Side by side** (heading on one side,
  questions on the other).
- **Contact prompt:** text and a button linking to your contact page.
- **Add FAQ structured data:** helps search engines show your questions in
  results. Use it on one FAQ section per page.

## 404 page

Shown when a page isn't found, with a search box and links back to the
shop and home page.

## Password page

Shown while your store is password protected (**Online Store →
Preferences**). Set a **Logo**, a **Heading** and whether to **Show email
signup**.

## Gift card page

The page customers see when they receive a gift card, with the gift card
code, its balance, a QR code and a button to add it to Apple Wallet.
Shoppers can send a gift card to someone else from the product page, using
the gift card recipient form in the **Buy buttons** block.
