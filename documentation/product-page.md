# Product page

Open a product page in the theme editor to edit the **Product information**
section and its blocks. Changes apply to every product that uses the
template. To give some products a different layout, create a new product
template and assign it in the product's admin page.

## Style

**Style:** **Use theme setting**, **Classic**, **Modern** or **Catalogue**.

- **Catalogue** (the Monograph preset): numbered figure labels on the images (FIG. 01,
  FIG. 02 …), a line with the product type and SKU reference, and numbered
  product notes.
- **Modern:** rounded tabs and a softer sticky add to cart bar.
- **Classic:** a plain layout without the catalogue details.

## Media gallery

- **Layout:** Stacked, Grid, Carousel, or Carousel with thumbnails.
- **Media aspect ratio** and **Media width**
- **Enable image zoom:** opens images full screen, where shoppers can zoom in.

## Product details

- **Keep details visible while scrolling** and **Spacing**

## Blocks

Add, remove and reorder these blocks in the Product information section.

| Block | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Heading**, **Text** | Product title, vendor or any text. |
| **Price** | Price with a sale badge (**None**, **"Sale" label** or **Percentage saved**), an optional currency code and the tax and shipping note. |
| **Variant picker** | **Buttons** or **Dropdown**. **Show swatches** uses the color or image swatches set on the option's category metafield. |
| **Buy buttons** | Add to cart, an optional quantity selector, dynamic checkout buttons such as Shop Pay, Shop Pay Installments when it's available, and a gift card recipient form for gift card products. |
| **Pickup availability** | Shows whether the variant can be picked up in store. Needs local pickup set up in **Settings → Shipping and delivery**. |
| **Sticky add to cart** | A bar fixed to the bottom of the screen with the product, price and an add to cart button. It appears once the main buy buttons scroll out of view. |
| **Size guide** | A link that opens a size guide. Can show a **ring size chart**, your own **Text**, and the content of a **Page**. With **Only show on products with sizes**, the link appears only when the product has a "Size" option. |
| **Personalization** | Optional **engraving** (with a character limit and a short note such as "Free") and a **gift note**. Values are saved with the cart line, so you see them on the order. **Only for products tagged** limits engraving to products with that tag. |
| **Delivery estimate** | "Arrives Mon, Nov 30 – Wed, Dec 2", counted in business days from the shopper's local time. Set the earliest and latest days, the **Same-day order cutoff (hour)**, and weekend days to skip. **Show order-by countdown** adds "Order within 2h 10m" before the dates. It's an estimate shown to shoppers and doesn't change your shipping rates. |
| **Inventory status** | "Only X left" at or below the **Low stock threshold**, with an optional stock bar. Needs inventory tracking. |
| **Product specs** | A list of details such as material and size. Connect each **Spec** row to a product metafield so every product shows its own value; rows with no value are hidden. |
| **Product tabs** | Tabs for details such as the description, materials and care, or shipping. Each **Tab** can show the product description or its own text. |
| **Accordion** | A collapsible row of text. Connect a product metafield to show different content for each product. |
| **Complete the set** | The product plus up to three complementary products, each with a checkbox, a running total and one button to add them all. Set up complementary products in the **Search & Discovery** app. Hidden when there are none. |
| **Ask a question** | A link that opens a short contact form. The message reaches you as a contact form email with the product and its link. |
| **Breadcrumbs** | Home / Collection / Product. **Use the product's first collection** when shoppers didn't arrive from a collection. |
| **List**, **Tags**, **Button**, **Payment icons**, **Share** | Supporting details, such as "Free insured shipping" with a check mark, or "Secure checkout" with payment icons. |
| **Custom Liquid** and app blocks | Add app content, such as reviews. |

## Product recommendations

The **Product recommendations** section shows **Related products** or
**Complementary products**. Complementary products are set up per product
in the Search & Discovery app. The section stays hidden when there's nothing
to recommend.

## Recently viewed

The **Recently viewed** section shows products the shopper looked at
recently, newest first. It's hidden until there's something to show.

## Quick view

When **Show quick add** is on (Theme settings → Product cards), products
with options open a quick view with a carousel and the variant picker. Edit
its **Layout**, **Media aspect ratio** and **Spacing** in the **Quick view**
section.
