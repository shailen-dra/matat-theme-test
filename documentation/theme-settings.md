# Theme settings

Open the theme editor and click the paintbrush icon to see the settings
that apply to the whole store.

## Favicon

**Favicon image:** the small icon shown in browser tabs. Displayed at 32 × 32px.

## Typography

- **Body:** the font for body text, buttons and labels.
- **Headings:** the font for headings.

Both use Shopify's font library. The Colophon footer names these fonts in its
bottom bar (see [Footer](footer.md)).

## Layout

- **Page width:** **Narrow** or **Wide**. Sets the maximum width of page content.
- **Page margin:** the space between the content and the edge of the screen.
- **Show back to top button:** shows a button that returns shoppers to the
  top of long pages.

## Colors

- **Color schemes:** each scheme sets a background, text and accent color.
  Sections that have a **Color scheme** setting can use any of them.
- **Page color scheme:** the scheme used for the page background and text.
- **Input corner radius:** rounds the corners of text fields.

## Animations

- **Reveal sections on scroll:** sections fade in as shoppers scroll.
- **Parallax images:** large banner and section images drift gently as
  shoppers scroll.

Both are turned off automatically for shoppers who prefer reduced motion.

## Buttons

- **Button corner radius:** rounds the corners of buttons.
- **Panel and tag corner radius:** at **0**, panels, tags, labels and icon
  buttons (such as arrows, close buttons and quick add) are square. Above 0,
  glass collage tiles, countdown tiles and testimonial cards are rounded,
  tags and labels become pills, and icon buttons become circles. The
  Monograph preset uses 0.

## Style

These settings give the theme its overall look. Individual sections can
override some of them in their own settings.

- **Section headings:**
  - **Classic:** a centered eyebrow, heading and subtitle.
  - **Chapter:** each section opens with a full-width rule, a running number
    (01, 02 …) beside the eyebrow, and a left-aligned heading, like the
    chapters of a printed catalogue. The numbers count down each page.
- **Sticky header:** **Bar** or **Glass bubble**. Applies when the header is
  set to stick.
- **Footer:** **Classic**, **Floating card** or **Colophon**. See [Footer](footer.md).
- **Product page:** **Classic**, **Modern** or **Catalogue**. See [Product page](product-page.md).

## Product cards

- **Card style:**
  - **Classic:** image, title and price.
  - **Modern:** rounded corners and floating badges.
  - **Ledger:** each piece is numbered and has a material line and a ruled
    price row, with product grids ruled into one plate, like a catalogue.
    Ledger also turns the cart into an order slip (see [Cart](cart.md)).
- **Show quick add:** adds a quick add button to product cards.
  Single-option products are added straight to the cart; others open a
  quick view to choose options.
- **Show swatches:** for products with a **Color** or **Metal** option, shows
  swatches on the card. Hovering a swatch shows that variant's image.
- **Show "New" badge for:** the number of days after a product is published
  that it shows a "New" badge. Set to **0** to turn the badge off.

Product cards also show **Sale** and **Sold out** badges automatically.

## Privacy consent

- **Require consent on forms:** adds a required checkbox to the contact,
  newsletter, ask a question, comment and password page forms.
- **Consent text:** leave blank to link to your privacy policy, which you
  manage in **Settings → Policies**.

## Social media

Add the full link to your profile on Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, TikTok,
YouTube, WhatsApp or X (Twitter). Icons appear wherever the **Social links**
block is used, such as the footer.
