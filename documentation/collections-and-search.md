# Collections and search

## Collection pages

Open a collection page in the theme editor to edit the **Collection** section.

### Collection intro

- **Show collection description**
- **Layout:** **Image beside text**, or **Banner with text on the image**.
- **Show collection image**, and a **Fallback banner image** for
  collections without their own image. Set each collection's image in
  **Products → Collections**.
- For the banner: **Height**, **Image overlay**, **Text color**, and the
  text's horizontal and vertical position.

### Plates and index views

With **Show plates and index views** on, shoppers can switch between:

- **Plates:** the photo grid of product cards.
- **Index:** a numbered list, like a price list, with each piece's number,
  name, material and price. On desktop, the hovered piece's photo follows the
  cursor.

**Default view** sets which one shoppers see first. Their choice is
remembered as they browse.

The material line comes from a **Metal** or **Material** option on the
product. Products without one show their type.

### Products

**Products per page**, **Columns on desktop**, **Columns on mobile**,
**Image ratio**, **Show second image on hover** and **Show vendor**.

### Promo tile

**Show promo tile** adds an image tile with a heading and button to the
product grid on the first page. Set its **Position in grid** and whether it
should **Span two columns**.

### Filtering and sorting

- **Enable filtering:** filters come from the free Shopify **Search &
  Discovery** app, where you choose filters such as availability, price,
  product type, size or metal.
- **Filter layout:** **Sidebar** or **Drawer**. Small screens always use
  the drawer.
- **Enable sorting**

## Collections list page

The **Collections grid** section on the collections list page
(`/collections`) shows every collection.

- **Layout:**
  - **Grid:** image cards with the collection name and product count.
  - **Contents list:** a numbered table of contents. Each row shows the
    number, collection name and product count. On desktop, the collection's
    photo follows the cursor; on mobile, each row shows a small image.
- **Columns on desktop**, **Columns on mobile** and **Image ratio** (Grid only)
- **Show product count**

## Search

### Search page

- **Popular searches:** terms shown before a search and when nothing is
  found. Separate terms with commas.
- **Show result type tabs:** All, Products, Articles and Pages.
- **Results per page**, **Columns on desktop**, **Columns on mobile**,
  **Image ratio**, **Show second image on hover** and **Show vendor**
- **Enable filtering** and **Enable sorting**
- **Show plates and index views** and **Default view:** search results can
  use the same plates and index views as collections. In the index, articles
  and pages are listed in the same numbered rows as products, labelled with
  their type.
- **Show suggested products:** products shown before a search and when
  nothing is found, from the **Suggested products collection**.

### Predictive search

With **Enable predictive search** on in the Header, the search box shows
products, collections, articles and pages as shoppers type.
