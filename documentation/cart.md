# Cart

## Cart drawer or cart page

**Theme settings → Cart → Cart type:**

- **Drawer:** a panel slides in from the side when shoppers open the cart or
  add a product, so they can keep shopping.
- **Page:** shoppers go to the cart page.

The cart page is always available at `/cart`, for example from the drawer's
**View cart** button.

## Order-slip style

With **Theme settings → Product cards → Card style** set to **Ledger**, the
cart page and drawer read as an order slip:

- each line shows its number (No. 01, No. 02 …) and the product type on a
  ruled line,
- the product's SKU is shown as a reference, for example "Ref. MJ-8101-01",
- the order summary is a ruled slip instead of a filled panel.

## Free shipping bar

**Free shipping threshold:** the order total for free shipping, in your
store's currency. The cart shows how much more the shopper needs to spend.
Set it to **0** to hide the bar.

The bar is a message only. Set up the free shipping rate itself in
**Settings → Shipping and delivery**, using the same amount.

## Product suggestions

**Show product suggestions in cart drawer** shows up to three products
based on Shopify's product recommendations for the first item in the cart.
Products already in the cart and sold-out products are left out.

## Gift wrap

**Gift wrap product** shows an "Add gift wrapping" checkbox in the cart that
adds this product. Create a product for gift wrapping with a single
variant and its price, then choose it here.

## Cart page settings

Open the cart page in the theme editor to edit the **Cart** section:

- **Show vendor**
- **Show order note:** lets shoppers add a note or gift message to the order.
- **Show dynamic checkout buttons:** such as Shop Pay or PayPal, when
  they're enabled in your payment settings.
- **Text below the checkout button:** for example your shipping and returns
  promise.
- **Empty cart:** **Suggested products** from a collection, with a
  **Heading** and **Link label**.

## Discounts

Shoppers enter discount codes at checkout. Automatic discounts and discount
codes already applied are shown in the cart.
