# Footer

The footer is a section group shown on every page. Open it from any page in
the theme editor.

## Style

**Style:** **Use theme setting**, **Classic**, **Floating card** or
**Colophon**. The theme setting is in **Theme settings → Style → Footer**.

- **Classic:** a full-width footer in your chosen colors.
- **Floating card:** a rounded panel inset from the edges of the page, with
  a glass newsletter card and round social buttons.
- **Colophon:** the back page of a printed catalogue. A heavy rule opens
  the footer, thin rules divide the columns, menu headings are numbered
  (01 Shop, 02 Help …), and the bottom bar names the fonts your store uses,
  for example "Set in Cormorant and Jost". On mobile the rules run across
  instead of down.

## Colors

**Background** and **Text color**.

## Columns

Add blocks to build the footer's columns:

- **Menu:** a **Heading** and a **Menu** from **Online Store → Navigation**.
- **Newsletter:** a sign-up form, **Underline with arrow** or **Boxed with
  button**. New subscribers are added to your customer list with email
  marketing consent.
- **Group:** combine blocks such as **Logo**, **Heading**, **Text** and
  **Social links** in one column.
- **Social links:** icons for the profiles you add in Theme settings →
  Social media.

## Layout

- **Make first column wider**
- **Show large wordmark:** shows the store name in large type above the
  bottom bar.

## Bottom bar

- **Show policy links:** links to the policies in **Settings → Policies**.
- **Show payment icons**
- **Show Follow on Shop:** lets customers follow your store in the Shop app.
  Requires Shop Pay.
- **Show country/region selector** and **Show language selector**
- **Show "Powered by Shopify"**
