# Home page sections

You can add any of these sections to the home page and to most other
templates. Click **Add section** in the theme editor's sidebar.

Most sections share a few settings:

- **Eyebrow**, **Heading** and **Subtitle:** the section's intro. Their look
  follows **Theme settings → Style → Section headings** (Classic or Chapter).
  In a heading, use italics to show a word in the accent color.
- **Color scheme** or **Tinted background:** the section's colors.
- **Top padding** and **Bottom padding**

## Slideshow

Full-width slides, each with an image, text and buttons.

- **Height:** Small, Medium, Large or Full screen.
- **Transition:** Slide, Fade or Fading boxes.
- **Show arrows**, **Auto-rotate slides** and **Change slides every**.
  Auto-rotation pauses on hover and focus, and never runs for shoppers who
  prefer reduced motion.
- **Slide** blocks: **Image**, an optional **Mobile image**, content position,
  **Text color** and an overlay. Add Heading, Text, Tags, Button and other
  blocks inside each slide.

## Hero banner

One large image with text and buttons. Settings for **Height**, **Mobile
layout** (text over or below the image), the overlay, content position,
**Maximum width** and **Animate on load**.

## Collection tiles

A row of collections, or any links, as image tiles. In Monograph it's used
for "Shop by category" and "Shop by metal".

- **Shape:** Circle, Square, Portrait or Arch.
- **Label position:** below the image or on it.
- **Columns on desktop**, **Mobile layout** (Swipe or Grid) and **Spacing**.
- **Collection tile** blocks: pick a **Collection**, or set your own
  **Heading**, **Link** and **Image**. **Swatch** shows a color or gradient
  instead of an image, which works well for metals or colors.

## Featured products

Products from one collection, using your product card style.

- **Collection** and **Products to show**
- **Columns on desktop**, **Columns on mobile**, **Image ratio**, **Show
  second image on hover** and **Show vendor**
- **Link label** and **Link:** leave the link empty to link to the collection.

## Featured product

One product with its full product details, as on the product page. Add and
arrange its blocks just like the product page. See [Product page](product-page.md).

## Shop the look

A photo with hotspots on the products in it, beside a list of those products
with add to cart buttons.

1. Choose the **Image**.
2. Add a **Hotspot** block for each product, choose the **Product**, and move
   the hotspot over the piece with **Horizontal position** and **Vertical
   position**.

## Image with text

An image beside text, with Heading, Text, List, Button and other blocks.
Settings for **Image ratio** (Portrait, Square, Landscape, Arch or Adapt to
image), **Image position**, **Image width**, and the content's position and
alignment.

## Collage

Up to several tiles in a mosaic, each showing an image, a product or a
collection.

- **Layout:** large tile first or last.
- **Label style:** Gradient, or Glass label (frosted glass, rounded by the
  **Panel and tag corner radius** setting).
- **Height** and **Spacing**
- **Tile** blocks: **Content** (Image, Product or Collection), with an
  optional **Image**, **Heading** and **Link**.

## Video

An uploaded video or a YouTube or Vimeo link, with an optional **Cover
image**. Links load only when shoppers press play. **Play as background**
plays an uploaded video muted on a loop, with a pause button.

## Countdown

A countdown to a date, for launches and sales.

- **End date** (YYYY-MM-DD) and **End time** (24-hour), in your store's time zone.
- **When the countdown ends:** show the **Ended message**, or hide the section.
- **Number tiles:** Outline, or Glass with flip.

Remember to update the end date, or the section shows the ended message.

## Testimonials

Customer quotes with star ratings.

- **Layout:** **Slider with arrows** or **Auto-scrolling row**. The
  auto-scrolling row pauses on hover; shoppers who prefer reduced motion see
  the slider.
- **Testimonial** blocks: **Quote**, **Star rating**, **Author**, **Detail**,
  an optional **Reviewed product** and **Photo**.

## Shoppable gallery

A grid of photos, such as social posts, that link to products.

- **Social handle** and **Profile link**, and **Columns on desktop**.
- **Photo** blocks: an **Image**, and a **Product** to show its name and
  price on hover, or a **Link**.

## Blog posts

The latest articles from a blog.

- **Blog**, **Articles to show**
- **Layout:**
  - **Cards:** a grid of article cards.
  - **Journal list:** numbered, ruled entries with the date, title and
    excerpt, and the photo at the end of each row.
- **Show featured image**, **Show date** and **Show excerpt**

## More sections

- **Marquee:** a scrolling line of short messages. Pauses on hover and stays
  still for shoppers who prefer reduced motion.
- **Press logos:** publication logos, shown in grayscale.
- **Trust badges:** short promises with icons, such as shipping and returns.
- **Timeline:** milestones with dates, text and images.
- **Comparison table:** compare your products with others, using "yes" and
  "no" for checks and crosses.
- **Before and after:** a slider comparing two images of the same size.
- **FAQ:** questions and answers. See [Blog and pages](blog-and-pages.md).
- **Store locations:** your stores' addresses, hours, phone numbers and a
  booking button.
- **Recently viewed:** products the shopper viewed recently. Hidden until
  there's something to show.
- **Custom section:** an empty section for your own combination of blocks.
- **Custom Liquid:** add app snippets or your own Liquid code.
- **Apps:** app blocks can be added to sections that support them.
