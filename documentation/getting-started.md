# Getting started

## Install the theme

1. In Shopify admin, go to **Online Store → Themes**.
2. Add Monograph from the Shopify Theme Store.
3. Monograph is added to your theme library. Click **Customize** to set it
   up before you publish, then **Publish** when you're ready.

You can try the theme for free in your store before you buy it.

## The Monograph preset

A preset is a starting look for the theme: its fonts, colors, styles and
the sections on its pages. Monograph's preset is made for jewelry and
accessory brands and is laid out like a printed catalogue: the index rail
header, ledger product cards, a catalogue product page, an order-slip cart,
Chapter section headings, a journal list and the Colophon footer, with
square corners throughout.

You can change everything afterwards.

## Making it your own

The catalogue look comes from a handful of settings, so you can take the
theme in a different direction without editing code. For a softer, more
modern store, for example:

- **Theme settings → Style:** set **Section headings** to **Classic**,
  **Sticky header** to **Glass bubble**, **Footer** to **Floating card** and
  **Product page** to **Modern**.
- **Theme settings → Product cards:** set **Card style** to **Modern**.
- **Theme settings → Buttons:** raise **Button corner radius** and **Panel
  and tag corner radius** for rounded buttons, panels and tags.
- **Header:** pick a horizontal **Desktop layout** instead of the index rail.
- **Typography** and **Colors:** choose your own fonts and color schemes.

See [Theme settings](theme-settings.md) for every option.

## Using the theme editor

- **Pages:** use the page picker at the top of the editor to switch between
  the home page, product pages, collections, the cart, blog posts and other
  templates.
- **Sections and blocks:** the left sidebar lists the sections on the page.
  Click **Add section** to add one, drag sections to reorder them, and open a
  section to add, remove or reorder its blocks.
- **Theme settings:** the paintbrush icon opens settings that apply to the
  whole store, such as fonts, colors and the cart.
- **Header and footer:** these are section groups shown on every page. Edit
  them from any page.

## A checklist for your first setup

1. **Logo and favicon:** Header → **Logo**, and Theme settings → Favicon.
2. **Menus:** create your main menu and footer menu in **Online Store →
   Navigation**, then pick them in the Header and Footer. See
   [Header and menus](header-and-menus.md).
3. **Colors and fonts:** Theme settings → Colors and Typography.
4. **Home page:** replace the preset's images and text with your own. See
   [Home page sections](home-page-sections.md).
5. **Product pages:** check the product template's blocks, for example the
   size guide, engraving and delivery estimate. See [Product page](product-page.md).
6. **Filters:** install the free Shopify **Search & Discovery** app to set up
   collection filters. See [Collections and search](collections-and-search.md).
7. **Cart:** set the free shipping threshold and, if you offer it, a gift wrap
   product. See [Cart](cart.md).
8. **Social links and policies:** Theme settings → Social media, and
   **Settings → Policies** in Shopify admin.

## Demo images

Images from the demo store aren't copied to your store when you install a
preset. Sections show placeholders until you choose your own images.
