# FAQ

## Setup

**Why doesn't my store look like the demo after installing?**
Demo images and products aren't copied to your store. Each section shows a
placeholder until you choose your own images, collections and products.
The layout, fonts, colors and styles match the Monograph preset.

**Can I make the theme look less like a catalogue?**
Yes. The catalogue look comes from a few settings under **Theme settings →
Style**, **Product cards** and **Buttons**, plus the Header's **Desktop
layout**. See [Making it your own](getting-started.md#making-it-your-own).

**Where do I set up my menus?**
In **Online Store → Navigation**. Then choose them in the Header's **Menu**
setting and in the footer's **Menu** blocks.

## Header and navigation

**How do I get the numbered index down the side of the page?**
In the Header, set **Desktop layout** to **Index rail (vertical on
desktop)**. On mobile it becomes a numbered strip under the header and an
index-style menu drawer.

**My index rail looks crowded. What should I do?**
Keep the main menu to about four to eight top-level items with short names,
and put longer lists in submenus.

**How do I add a mega menu?**
Add a **Mega menu** block to the Header and type the name of the top-level
menu item it belongs to. See [Header and menus](header-and-menus.md#mega-menus).

## Products and collections

**How do I add filters to collection pages?**
Install the free Shopify **Search & Discovery** app and choose your
filters there. Then make sure **Enable filtering** is on in the Collection
section.

**Where does the material on product cards come from?**
From a product option named **Metal** or **Material**. Products without
one show their product type.

**How do I show color or metal swatches?**
Turn on **Show swatches** in Theme settings → Product cards for options
named Color or Metal. On the product page, the **Variant picker** uses the
swatches set on the option's category metafield.

**How do I turn off the "New" badge?**
Set **Theme settings → Product cards → Show "New" badge for** to **0**.

**How do I offer engraving only on some products?**
In the **Personalization** block, type a product tag in **Only for products
tagged**, then add that tag to the products that can be engraved.

**How do I set up "Complete the set" or complementary recommendations?**
Choose complementary products for each product in the **Search &
Discovery** app. The blocks stay hidden for products without any.

**Does the delivery estimate change my shipping rates?**
No. It's an estimate shown to shoppers. Set your rates in **Settings →
Shipping and delivery**.

## Cart and checkout

**The free shipping bar doesn't match my shipping rates.**
The bar only shows a message. Use the same amount in **Theme settings →
Cart → Free shipping threshold** and in your free shipping rate in
**Settings → Shipping and delivery**.

**Can shoppers enter a discount code in the cart?**
Shoppers enter discount codes at checkout. Discounts already applied show in
the cart.

**Can I customize the checkout?**
Checkout is managed by Shopify, not the theme. Customize it in **Settings →
Checkout**.

## Languages and markets

**Can I sell in more than one language or currency?**
Yes. Add languages in **Settings → Languages** and countries in **Settings →
Markets**. The header and footer show language and country selectors when
there's more than one to choose from. The theme includes English text; use
the Translate & Adapt app to translate it.

**Does the theme support right-to-left languages?**
The theme includes right-to-left layout styles, but no right-to-left
translations. Test your store carefully if you publish a right-to-left
language.

## Updates and changes

**How do I update the theme?**
When a new version is released, you'll see an update notice in **Online
Store → Themes**. Updating adds a new copy of the theme with your settings
and content. Check the copy, then publish it.

**Will I lose my changes when I update?**
Settings and content made in the theme editor carry over. Changes to the
theme's code don't, so keep a note of any code edits.

**Can I edit the theme's code?**
Yes, but duplicate the theme first, and note that code changes aren't
covered by our support. For custom work, you can [hire a Shopify
Partner](https://www.shopify.com/partners/directory).
